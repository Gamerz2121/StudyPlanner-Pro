Your data is stored using Firebase Firestore and Authentication services. We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

5. Your Choices

You can:

Our Service is intended for students of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the application interface.
